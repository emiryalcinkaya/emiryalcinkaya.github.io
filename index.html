Privacy Policy for Asteroid Escape: Space Run

Last updated: April 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Asteroid Escape: Space Run.

1. Information We Collect
Asteroid Escape does not require account creation and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number inside the game.

However, the app uses third-party advertising services. These services may automatically process certain device-related data, which may include:
- Device identifiers, such as advertising identifiers
- IP address
- Device and operating system information
- Ad interaction data
- Diagnostic and performance-related data

2. Third-Party Services
Asteroid Escape: Space Run uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may process data to provide, measure, and improve ads.

You can learn more here:
Google Ads Policy

3. How Information May Be Used
Data processed by third-party advertising services may be used for purposes such as:
- Serving ads
- Measuring ad performance
- Preventing fraud and abuse
- Improving service reliability and performance

4. Personalized Advertising and Consent
If required by applicable law, users in certain regions, including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, may be asked to provide consent for personalized advertising and related data use.

5. Data Security
We do not directly store or manage personal user accounts or profiles in the app. Data processed through third-party services is handled according to those providers’ own policies and security practices.

6. Children’s Privacy
Asteroid Escape is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

7. Your Choices
Depending on your device settings and region, you may be able to manage advertising preferences, tracking permissions, and privacy choices through your device settings.

8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

9. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or need support, you can contact us at:
asteroidescape.support@gmail.com
